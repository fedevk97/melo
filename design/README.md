# Design assets

- [MELO.md](MELO.md) documents Melo's colour, type, spacing, and interaction system.
- [melo-mark.svg](melo-mark.svg) is the vector master of the M.
- [melo-logo.png](melo-logo.png) is the 512 px logo on Melo's black.
- The README art (logos, hero, screen strips and app icons) lives in [docs/assets/img/readme](../docs/assets/img/readme).
- [Doto-OFL.txt](Doto-OFL.txt) contains the typeface license.

The app interface uses neutral surfaces and typography while keeping music artwork in its original colours. The logo and banner are identity assets; they do not represent an app screenshot.
